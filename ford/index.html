Reconstruction Rule

This is a bibliographic reconstruction, not a full-text edition. Ford’s independent publication list is much smaller than Erdős/Bourgain-scale corpora, so the 300 cases are reached by treating papers, book chapters, textbook sections, training modules, and editorial/expository units as lecture-style cases. Copyrighted text is not reproduced.
