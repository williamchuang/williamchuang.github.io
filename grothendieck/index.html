Research-page premise

Grothendieck's work repeatedly changes the arena before solving the problem: from spaces to functors, from varieties to schemes, from points to topoi, from examples to universal properties, from theorems to foundations, and from isolated proofs to seminar-scale infrastructures.

Methodological rule: later vocabulary is used as retrospective labeling only. The reconstruction does not assume mathematical developments after Grothendieck's active period as premises for his own derivations.
