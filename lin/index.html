Research Reconstruction

Lin's method is reconstructed as a union of hydrodynamic-stability spectral analysis, critical-layer asymptotics, turbulence closure, wave mechanics, rotating-disk self-gravity, and institutional applied-mathematics pedagogy.

The 300 cases are section-level and paper-level reconstructions. They do not claim that Lin published 300 separate monographs; rather, they treat papers, chapters, lectures, and coherent sections as lecture-style cases suitable for reverse-engineering method.
