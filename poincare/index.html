Henri Poincaré’s Work Algorithms

A 300-case reconstruction of Poincaré’s mathematical and scientific workflow across Fuchsian functions, qualitative differential equations, celestial mechanics, topology, mathematical physics, electrodynamics, probability, and philosophy of science. Each paper, book chapter, report, lecture, or section-style unit is treated as one case. Histogram percentages are overlapping case prevalence and need not sum to 100%.

Reconstruction Rule

This is a bibliographic reconstruction, not a full-text edition. The source spine is Poincaré’s publication bibliography, collected works, mathematical monographs, public philosophical books, and lecture/report materials. The method deliberately treats papers, book chapters, reports, and section-style units as comparable lecture cases, then tags each case with overlapping strategies.
