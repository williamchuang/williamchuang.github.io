Reconstruction method

The unit is not “what secret instruction did Roosevelt give?” It is a public-source decision unit: situation, uncertainty, question ladder, historical move, likely artifact, and caution. Case units are synthesized from public/declassified sources and secondary public records; they are prompts for judgment, not operational instructions.

Core thesis

Roosevelt’s method combined elite access, historical training, OSS memory, Arabist area knowledge, liaison habits, field confidence, and early CIA covert-action authority. The strength was rapid compression of political crisis into action. The danger was deniability, allied-policy capture, partner overconfidence, sovereignty injury, and mythologized success.

Case unit

Each row asks what a historically bounded reader should ask first: authority, source quality, British and local partner motives, legitimacy, abort threshold, documentation, and downstream political memory.

Ethical overlay

The page treats the 1953 Iran coup and its long aftermath as a warning about covert-action temptation. Success and failure both count as evidence, and the most important strategy is often a negative one: do not turn this case into a template.

Decision tree: reading Roosevelt as method

1. Identify the pressure source

Is the case driven by oil, Cold War fear, British liaison, Iranian constitutional conflict, presidential desire, field confidence, or later memoir?

2. Locate authority

Find the approval path, the policy owner, the field lane, and the record a later historian would need.

3. Separate evidence from interest

Distinguish Iranian political reality, British policy preference, U.S. threat perception, and Roosevelt’s own retrospective narration.

4. Stress local partners

Ask what each partner wants, whether they can deliver, and what they may do after support creates new incentives.

5. Force the abort question

When a plan fails, require a stop/continue review rather than letting sunk cost, charisma, or institutional pride decide.

6. Extend the clock

Do not end the case on August 19, 1953. Continue through stabilization, repression, memoir, declassification, 1979 memory, and current U.S.-Iran mistrust.

Limits and ethics

Not a how-to page

No row gives tactical guidance for covert action, recruitment, bribery, crowd mobilization, evasion, sabotage, or clandestine communication. The page abstracts the case into governance and historical judgment.

Public-source humility

Many records are partial, redacted, self-protective, or retrospective. The page is a reconstruction of decision logic, not a claim to complete archival possession.

Central warning

The strongest lesson is non-replicability. A covert-action “success” can still damage sovereignty, legitimacy, institutional restraint, and future foreign relations.
