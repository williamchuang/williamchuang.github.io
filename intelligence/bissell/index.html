Reconstruction method

The unit of analysis is not a secret instruction. It is a public-source decision unit: situation, uncertainty, why-question ladder, likely management move, artifact, strategy tags, and guardrail. The reconstruction borrows the page shape from the Casey, Dulles, and Donovan templates while shifting the content to Bissell’s distinctive combination of economist, program builder, technical-collection entrepreneur, covert-action manager, and failure case.

Core thesis

Bissell’s recurring method joined systems economics, small elite networks, public-private technical acceleration, special-access project management, and presidentially relevant intelligence. Its brilliance is visible in U-2/CORONA-style innovation; its danger is visible when the same compressed, secret, confidence-heavy style entered political warfare and Cuba.

Case unit

Each row asks what Bissell’s situation demanded: the information gap, the instrument, the authority, the contractor or partner, the exploitation chain, the exposure scenario, and the later investigator’s reconstruction.

Modern overlay

The page adds guardrails that Bissell’s era often underweighted: policy-intelligence separation, written authority, independent red-team review, partner legitimacy, public accountability, and blowback accounting.

Decision tree: reading Bissell as method

1. Define the gap

Is the problem economic, administrative, technical collection, covert policy, partner-force viability, or post-failure accountability?

2. Choose the instrument

Map whether the answer is a budget line, contractor compact, scientific review, overhead platform, covert option, or investigative record.

3. Locate authority

Identify presidential approval, DCI/DDP authority, interagency review, contractor responsibility, and the later oversight lane.

4. Test the assumption stack

Find the premise that must be true: platform survivability, local uprising, deniability, air support, scientific feasibility, or political appetite.

5. Build the artifact

Bissell’s habit is artifact-producing: memo, dashboard, requirement, advisory report, contract gate, exploitation chain, or postmortem file.

6. Price exposure

Ask what happens if the system is detected, captured, declassified, investigated, or remembered by the people affected by it.

Systems-economist problem framing

mission problem → constraints → incentives → institutional design

When a national-security problem looks technical, first map the economic and administrative system that makes it solvable or unsolvable.

Questions, move, artifact, failure mode
Why questions

These demonstrations show how to use the strategy cards without turning the historical material into a how-to manual.

AQUATONE / U-2 as technical sprint

1

Define the Soviet military-intelligence gap without assuming the aircraft is the answer.

2

Build the scientist-contractor compact, then add independent challenge and presidential exposure review.

3

Plan exploitation of photographs before celebrating collection.

4

Record that the platform’s political window is temporary.

CORONA as failure-tolerant successor

1

Treat repeated launch and recovery failures as a structured learning series.

2

Keep the political advantage of satellites visible: less pilot-capture exposure than overflight aircraft.

3

Coordinate CIA, Air Force, contractors, and scientists through named accountability lanes.

4

Move success into the archive later so public trust can catch up to secret innovation.

Bay of Pigs as assumption failure

1

List the core assumptions: local uprising, partner viability, deniability, air support, and presidential escalation appetite.

2

Kill-test each assumption before launch, not during crisis.

3

Make support dependencies explicit and require written go/no-go decisions.

4

After failure, compare memoir, internal investigation, committee record, and declassified files.

OXCART/A-12 as successor hedge

1

Start the successor before the U-2 window closes.

2

Use external scientific advice to compare designs and survivability claims.

3

Price the secrecy, cost, and diplomatic-exposure burden of the new platform.

4

Prevent technical elegance from becoming a substitute for strategic necessity.

Limits & ethics

Not mind-reading

This is not a claim about Bissell’s private thoughts. It is a structured reconstruction of recurring public decision patterns.

Not operational guidance

The page avoids clandestine procedures, surveillance methods, sabotage instructions, recruitment tradecraft, and tactical covert-action guidance.

Failure is evidence

Bay of Pigs is central because failures expose assumptions, authority gaps, and institutional incentives more clearly than successes do.

Use with source humility

Official histories, memoirs, oral histories, and later investigations disagree. This page treats disagreement as a prompt for better questions, not as a defect to smooth away.
