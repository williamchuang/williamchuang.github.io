Reconstruction method

The unit of analysis is a public-source decision unit: situation, moral uncertainty, question ladder, Bernard-style move, artifact, skill family, and failure mode. The page uses Bernard’s life and writings as a structured prompt for judgment, not as mind-reading.

Core thesis

Bernard’s method joined ascetic credibility, affective theology, scriptural imagination, epistolary governance, elite-network counsel, institutional reform, and crisis intervention. The same habits created sanctity, reform energy, and serious moral hazards when charisma entered public mobilization.

Case unit

Each case asks what Bernard would likely ask first: Is this a problem of desire, rule, office, unity, doctrine, violence, reputation, or contemplation?

Ethical overlay

Success and failure are both methodological evidence. The Second Crusade and Templar materials are not romanticized; they are used to stress-test authority, rhetoric, crowd zeal, protection duties, and accountability.

01

Decision tree: reading Bernard as method

1. Locate the wound

Is the disorder in desire, pride, fear, ambition, doctrine, faction, luxury, violence, or busyness?

2. Identify the governing form

Which rule, vow, office, council, papal mandate, Scripture, or pastoral obligation governs the response?

3. Choose the corrective medium

Should the answer be silence, sermon, letter, rule, visitation, council speech, public rebuke, or withdrawal?

4. Bind zeal to charity

What good is sought, and how could zeal injure the body it claims to serve?

5. Preserve unity and truth together

What must not be compromised, and what can be conceded for peace?

6. Audit the future memory

What would monks, historians, victims, rulers, or later reformers need to know about the failure mode?

Limits and ethics

No private mind-reading

The cases reconstruct a plausible method from public materials and repeated patterns. They do not claim secret access to Bernard’s intentions.

No mobilization manual

Crusade, Templar, and crowd-preaching materials are analyzed as historical moral-risk cases, not as templates for modern coercion, propaganda, or violence.

Failure retained

The page preserves tension: Bernard as saint, reformer, mystic, counselor, polemicist, and controversial mobilizer. A useful method must retain the contradictions.
