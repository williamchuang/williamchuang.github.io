Reconstruction method

The unit of analysis is not “what secret procedure did Li Qiang use?” It is a public-source decision unit: historical situation, starting uncertainty, why-question ladder, likely work move, skill set, source anchor, and safety guardrail.

Core thesis

Li Qiang’s recurring method combined self-teaching, foreign-language technical extraction, radio engineering, organizational discipline, scarce-resource production management, talent protection, public infrastructure stewardship, and pragmatic industrial diplomacy.

Reading unit

Each case asks where the problem starts, what questions organize judgment, what artifact should result, and what later historians must not overclaim.

Ethical overlay

Communications intelligence and wartime industry are handled as historical accountability studies. Procedural details are abstracted into authority, evidence, risk, maintenance, training, and source criticism.

Limits, ethics, and use

Not a communications tradecraft page

Technical and clandestine details are deliberately abstracted. The purpose is historical interpretation, not replication.

Chinese-source translation caution

Several anchors are Chinese-language party-history or biographical sources. The English page should keep source type and translation choices visible.

Contested memory

Official biography, family recollection, party history, and later journalism preserve different kinds of truth and bias. Treat each as evidence with a confidence band.
