The Architecture

The core AI research program is grounded in the ASI Seed Architecture (U.S. provisional application 64/067,703, 2025): a seed architecture for artificial superintelligence combining global coordination (CEAS), causal reasoning (Ψ), and invariant geometric representation (GRAIL).

The four primary research systems—CEAS, GRAIL, MIA, and the Ψ-operator framework—are each summarized on dedicated pages; technical detail is available under NDA. Its confidentiality properties follow from group-invariant computation.

CEAS — Critical Entropy Attention

Global coordination channel with thermodynamic control of attention via explicit inverse temperature β. Physics-first training stability. U.S. provisional application 63/813,617.

GRAIL — Geometric Representation Algebra

Group-invariant neural computation on curved manifolds. Cryptomorphic twin models; automorphic attention. U.S. provisional applications 63/773,441 and 64/067,703.

MIA — Metric-Invariant Architecture

Replaces scalar arithmetic with invariant-first group operations. Geometric error correction; designed for 28–65 nm processes and RISC-V and FPGA targets. U.S. provisional application 63/901,369.

Ψ-Operator Framework

Causal operator framework: intervention and counterfactual reasoning with explicit, checkable identification criteria; constrained inverse design.
