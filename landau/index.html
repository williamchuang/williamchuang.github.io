Landau's Work Algorithms

A reverse-engineering of the Landau theoretical-physics style from the Landau–Lifshitz Course of Theoretical Physics and the Landau theoretical minimum tradition. The page reconstructs 33 probable strategies as a decision tree, then tests them against a 300-case corpus. Volume I Mechanics is represented at full section resolution: all 52 numbered sections are included because it is the compact variational and Hamiltonian spine of the course.

Methodological rule: the reconstruction treats post-Landau developments only as external commentary. The decision tree uses the assumptions and mathematical physics tools available to Landau's school and to the Course tradition, not later standard-model or modern condensed-matter frameworks as premises.

05Source Spine and Copyright Note

Source spine

The page uses the ten-volume Landau–Lifshitz Course of Theoretical Physics as the organizing corpus, plus the historical Landau theoretical minimum tradition. It uses chapter and section titles, derived summaries, and methodological reconstructions; it does not reproduce textbook prose.

Core source checkpoints: ten-volume Course description; Volume I Mechanics contents; Volume II and III table-of-contents summaries; Volume IV, VIII, and X table-of-contents summaries; public summaries of the theoretical minimum tradition.

Interpretive caveat

Landau initiated the Course and shaped its style, but the written series was produced collaboratively with Lifshitz and later contributors. The algorithmic reconstruction should be read as a reconstruction of the Landau-school theoretical style, not as a claim about private mental steps in any single author’s mind.
