Reconstruction Rule

Euclid’s surviving corpus is not a modern journal-paper corpus. This page therefore treats propositions, book sections, theorem clusters, constructions, and transmitted attributed-work units as “lecture cases.” The 300-case corpus is a structured reconstruction from Elements, Data, Optics, Phaenomena, and cautiously attributed or lost works, not a claim of 300 distinct published papers.
