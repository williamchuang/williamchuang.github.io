Reconstruction Rule

This is a bibliographic and methodological reconstruction, not a full-text edition and not a claim that Bowen wrote 300 distinct papers. Bowen's career was brief but unusually concentrated; therefore the 300 cases expand the source spine into theorem-level, lemma-level, chapter-level, survey-level, and proof-module units. The goal is to infer reusable work algorithms from the structure of his results without reproducing copyrighted source text.
Core spine: topological entropy, Axiom A diffeomorphisms, Markov partitions, periodic points and measures, symbolic dynamics for hyperbolic systems and flows, equilibrium states, Bowen-Ruelle Axiom A flows, CBMS synthesis, interval entropy, Couette-flow modeling, and quasi-circle dimension theory.
