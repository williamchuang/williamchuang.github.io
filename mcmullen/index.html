Core thesis

McMullen’s method repeatedly converts a difficult object into the neighboring geometry where its invariant is measurable: an algorithm becomes a rational map, a surface map becomes a hyperbolic 3-manifold, a billiard becomes a translation surface, and entropy becomes a spectral or arithmetic datum.

Deep reading unit

Each case is read as a pipeline: object → correct language → deformation or flow → invariant → table/example → cross-domain transfer → rigidity, classification, construction, or universality statement.

Why this style
